## Problem

The All characters page currently displays all characters in a flat list without any grouping or sorting by series. Users need to manually scan through all characters to find those belonging to a specific series, which is inefficient when managing characters across multiple animated series.

## Solution

Add sorting/filtering capability to the All characters page that allows characters to be grouped or sorted by their associated series. This could be implemented as:
- A dropdown filter to show characters from a specific series
- Grouped display with series as section headers
- Sortable columns in a table view
